# Elarindor Visual Reference Registry

This registry identifies creator-approved Authoritative Visual
References (AVRs), their roles, scopes, fidelity requirements, and
supersession status.

The actual image files may not be stored in GPT Knowledge.

If a listed reference is required for fidelity and the actual image is
not available to the image generator, request it from the creator before
generation.

A registry entry does not make incidental details in an image
authoritative. Each AVR controls only the scope explicitly assigned to
it.

## Registry Field Standard

Each AVR should use the following fields:

-   **Type**
-   **Subject**
-   **Status**
-   **Role**
-   **Scope**
-   **Required for Fidelity**
-   **Not Automatically Authoritative For**
-   **Generator Input**
-   **Notes**

Use **None established** where no supersession relationship exists. Use
**Not separately established** where a field has not yet been defined
rather than inventing one.

## Elarindor

### Elarindor_Initial_Concept.png

-   **Type:** World Map / Geographic Concept AVR
-   **Subject:** Elarindor
-   **Status:** Authoritative Visual Reference
-   **Role:** Geographic and world-level visual reference
-   **Scope:** Controls only those geographic relationships, named
    regions, major terrain relationships, and other map features that
    are separately established as authoritative in current Elarindor
    lore.
-   **Required for Fidelity:** Yes when generating or modifying a map of
    Elarindor, or when a requested image depends materially upon
    established geographic relationships or the visual arrangement of
    multiple regions.
-   **Not Automatically Authoritative For:**
    -   exact coastlines
    -   exact borders or regional boundaries
    -   exact roads or routes
    -   exact settlement locations
    -   exact terrain placement
    -   map scale or distances
    -   generated labels or place names
    -   heraldry, symbols, quotations, or decorative text
    -   architecture depicted by settlement icons
    -   regional colors
    -   vegetation distribution
    -   any other incidental AI-generated map detail unless
        independently established by authoritative Elarindor material
-   **Generator Input:** Actual image must be provided by the creator
    when required for a generation and not otherwise established as
    generator input.
-   **Notes:** Provides the current creator-approved visual concept for
    Elarindor's overall mapped presentation and broad regional
    character. `Elarindor_Final_Concept.png` is referenced in
    \[[Elarindor](#elarindor)\] but is **not currently registered as an
    AVR**. Its presence or filename does not supersede this entry or
    grant it visual authority. If the Final Concept is intended to
    replace, supplement, or divide scope with this AVR, that
    relationship must be explicitly established by the creator and
    recorded here.

### Vaelyn_Thistletide_Katana.png

- **Type:** Artifact / Equipment AVR

- **Subject:** Vaelyn Thistletide's Katana

- **Status:** Authoritative Visual Reference

- **Role:** Exact visual identity of Vaelyn Thistletide's personal katana

- **Scope:** Controls the established visual design and recognizable identity of Vaelyn Thistletide's katana, including:
  - the blade's characteristic katana curvature and overall profile;
  - the established blade-to-hilt proportions;
  - the tsuba and visible fittings;
  - the ornate pale ivory hilt;
  - the hilt's sculptural form, carved ornamentation, and established visual arrangement;
  - the terminal treatment of the hilt;
  - the overall proportions, silhouette, and visual identity of the weapon.

- **Required for Fidelity:** Yes whenever Vaelyn Thistletide's katana is materially visible in an image. The weapon must be reconstructed from this reference rather than replaced with a generic katana, a merely similar ivory-hilted sword, or a newly invented variation.

- **Not Automatically Authoritative For:**
  - the sword's name, if any;
  - magical properties;
  - metallurgical composition;
  - exact physical measurements not independently established in written lore;
  - the cultural meaning or identity of individual carved motifs unless independently established;
  - the identity of the sword's maker;
  - the history or circumstances of its creation unless independently established;
  - the design or appearance of Vaelyn's Swordmaster's katana; the Swordmaster's weapon is a separate katana and is not required to share Vaelyn's hilt, ornamentation, fittings, carvings, or other individual weapon-design features.
  - incidental lighting, reflections, wear, shadows, viewing angle, or other scene-dependent presentation.

- **Generator Input:** The actual reference image must be available to the image generator whenever Vaelyn's katana is materially visible and preservation of its established design is required. A prose description, remembered appearance, filename, or approximate reconstruction is not an acceptable substitute.

- **Notes:** This is Vaelyn Thistletide's specific personal weapon. Whenever the katana appears with Vaelyn, its established design must remain visually consistent with this AVR. Scene-dependent presentation may vary naturally with pose, perspective, lighting, partial occlusion, whether the weapon is drawn or sheathed, and other circumstances, but those variations must not redesign the weapon.

  The weapon should remain unmistakably a katana and should retain the established curved blade and ornate ivory hilt visible in the AVR.

  The reference establishes the sword itself only. Incidental details belonging to the surrounding concept-art scene, Vaelyn's clothing or pose, other characters, architecture, generated writing or symbols, lighting, and environment are outside this AVR's scope.

  If a dedicated isolated reference image of the katana is created from the currently approved visual design, that isolated image should be used as the generator-facing reference for this AVR rather than relying upon the full training-scene image.

## Valdari

### Valdari_Anatomy_Proportion_Study.png

- **Type:** Specialized AVR

- **Subject:** Valdari

- **Status:** Authoritative Visual Reference

- **Role:** Species anatomy and proportions

- **Scope:** Established Valdari anatomy and proportions

- **Required for Fidelity:** Yes when Valdari anatomy, body proportions, or scale are materially visible and the study is necessary to avoid design drift.

- **Not Automatically Authoritative For:** Individual character identity, clothing, occupation, culture, pigmentation range, or incidental presentation details not independently established.

- **Generator Input:** Actual image must be available to the generator when required for fidelity.

- **Notes:** Controls established Valdari anatomy and proportions within its registered scope. The height labels and population measurements depicted in the current image predate the current stature decisions in the [[Valdari Race Bible]] and are no longer authoritative for Valdari height. Current written stature rules control scale until this AVR is rebuilt or replaced. Pigmentation visible in the study does not define the full Valdari population range.

- **Notes:** This AVR depicts the specific katana commissioned for Vaelyn Thistletide by her Swordmaster. Its distinctive visual design belongs to Vaelyn's personal commissioned weapon and should not be generalized into a standard design for the Swordmaster's katana or for other swords of the martial tradition. Vaelyn's Swordmaster also uses a katana, but the appearance of the Swordmaster's individual weapon remains separately determined and is outside this AVR's scope.

### valdari_ear_anatomy_guide.png

-   **Type:** Specialized species visual-control reference

-   **Subject:** Valdari

-   **Status:** Current working reference

-   **Role:** Species-specific ear-anatomy control

-   **Scope:** Current visual guidance for established Valdari ear anatomy: a fundamentally human-like ear with a subtle species-specific modification concentrated in the upper portion.

-   **Required for Fidelity:** Yes when Valdari ears are materially visible and a generator-facing anatomy reference is needed to prevent drift toward generic pointed fantasy ears.

-   **Not Automatically Authoritative For:** Other anatomy, individual identity, facial structure, clothing, hairstyle, pigmentation, jewelry, environment, typography, decorative elements, or generated lore shown in the guide.

-   **Generator Input:** Use `artwork/species/valdari/valdari_ear_anatomy_guide.png` together with the current written ear anatomy in the [[Valdari Race Bible]].

-   **Notes:** Preferred current generator-facing Valdari ear reference. The written Race Bible controls the anatomical rule: human-like base anatomy, only slightly longer than a typical human ear, upper rim/helix flowing gently backward in a shallow arc, and a softly rounded terminal shape rather than a sharp point. The former `Valdari_Ears.png` registry reference is superseded. Its historical visual direction is retained at `artwork/species/valdari/valdari-ear-design-historical-reference.png` and should not be used as the primary ear-control reference when the current guide is available.

### Valdari Adult Scale
Visual references should use approximately **3'0" for a typical adult female and 3'2" for a typical adult male.** Children, conversely, need genuinely immature anatomy and proportions.
