# 1. Core Identity

The **Valdari** are a diminutive, exceptionally long-lived people native to one of the major continents of **Elarindor**.

At first glance, outsiders frequently mistake adult Valdari for children. Their small stature, soft facial structure, large expressive eyes, and unusually youthful appearance encourage the comparison. The resemblance is superficial.

An adult Valdari possesses the fully develop body, intellect, emotional maturity, experience, and bearing of an adult. Their people simply age differently.

The Valdari are characterized not by physical power or conquest, but by **patience**, **observation**, **craftsmanship**, **accumulated knowledge**, **community**, and **stewardship**.

They are lifelong learners and caretakers. Across their varied communities and ways of life, the Valdari place great value on knowledge cultivated patiently over time and passed from one generation to the next.

The Valdari do not regard nature as something outside themselves that must be protected from civilization.

They regard themselves as participants within it.

> *The Valdari are not defined by what they do, but by how they live.*

# 3. Physical Biology

## 3.1 Stature

**Status: Working Canon**

Valdari are among the smaller humanoid peoples of Elarindor.

The Valdari are a diminutive humanoid people. Fully grown adults generally range from **2'10" to 3'4" (86-102 cm)** in height.

Adult females average approximately **3'0" (91 cm),** while adult males average approximately **3'2" (97 cm).** These figures represent population averages rather than rigid sex-based limits; individual male and female heights overlap considerably.

Despite their small status, adult Valdari possess fully mature humanoid proportions. They should not resemble human children, chibi figures, or disproportionately large-headed fantasy smallfolk.

At approximately **3'2" (97 cm),** Vaelyn is taller than the average Valdari woman, not an unusually large Valdari.

Vaelyn's height is an individual characteristic and should not be used as the standard height for Valdari women.

> ![[Valdari_Adult_and_Human_Height_Comparison.png]]

## 3.2 Proportions

Valdari should **never be depicted as human children simply scaled down**.

Their bodies are those of physically mature adults belonging to a small humanoid species.

Likewise, they should not use exaggerated fantasy-smallfolk proportions such as:

-   oversized heads,
-   extremely short limbs,
-   enormous hands or feet,
-   squat torsos,
-   exaggerated potbellies,
-   cartoonishly broad shoulders.

Their proportions should remain believable and internally consistent.

Adult Valdari have mature skeletal structures and mature adult body proportions despite retaining unusually youthful facial characteristics.

This distinction is essential to their visual identity.

# 4. Adult Appearance

## 4.1 Youthful Aging

The most immediately recognizable Valdari characteristic is their unusually youthful appearance.

Adult Valdari retain:

-   smooth facial contours,
-   soft features,
-   large expressive eyes,
-   relatively youthful skin,
-   rounded or gently heart-shaped faces,
-   little conventional visible facial aging.
This youthful appearance persists throughout adulthood, including at venerable ages and near the natural end of life.

This can make even experienced adult Valdari appear remarkably young to humans and other peoples.

However:

> **Youthful appearance does not mean prepubescent anatomy.**

Adult Valdari are fully physically mature.

Their adulthood should also be communicated through posture, expression, movement, clothing, occupation, confidence, and social context.

An adult Valdari scholar carrying centuries of accumulated knowledge should not look or behave like a human child merely because the scholar possesses a youthful face.

## 4.2 Facial Structure

There is no single Valdari face.

Common tendencies include:

-   soft facial contours,
-   expressive eyes,
-   relatively small noses,
-   gently rounded cheeks,
-   youthful skin,
-   subtle facial proportions.

These characteristics should remain within realistic variation.

Valdari can have long, round, heart-shaped, narrow, broad, or otherwise individually distinctive faces.

Facial Hair  
Valdari males do not grow facial hair. Beards, mustaches, sideburns, and other forms of facial hair are absent regardless of age. This remains true even among venerable Valdari males and is part of the species’ distinctive youthful facial appearance. Age and maturity should instead be communicated through bearing, expression, posture, clothing, occupation, and social context.

# 5. Ears

The ears are one of the most important visual identifiers of the Valdari.

Older descriptions referred to them simply as **slightly pointed**. This is superseded.

Valdari ears are fundamentally **human-like in structure**, but the upper portion of the ear gently sweeps backward.

The result creates a subtle **windswept or leaf-like silhouette**.

They are:

-   not long elven ears,
-   not sharply pointed,
-   not oversized,
-   not horizontally protruding,
-   not Hobbit-like.

The backward sweep should be immediately noticeable upon inspection but subtle enough that the ear still looks biologically plausible.

# 6. Eyes

Valdari commonly possess bright, expressive eyes with a naturally **clear, luminous, and unusually vivid quality**.

This luminosity is visual rather than literal. Valdari eyes do not normally glow or emit light. Instead, their irises possess unusual clarity, richness, and vividness.

Natural Valdari eye colors include shades of:
- teal,
- amber,
- blue,
- green.

These colors occur broadly throughout the Valdari population and are not currently associated with particular geographic regions or Valdari cultures.

Their eyes contribute significantly to the youthful impression outsiders receive, but should not be exaggerated into cartoon or anime proportions.

### Vaelyn Exception

Vaelyn Thistletide possesses **emerald-green eyes with a subtle ring of sparkling gold surrounding each pupil**.

This is a characteristic of **Vaelyn**, not a standard Valdari racial trait.

The unusual sparkling-gold rings should not be generalized from Vaelyn to the wider Valdari population.

Its significance remains unknown during the early portion of her story.

# 7. Hair

Valdari hair exhibits normal individual variation in:

-   color,
-   texture,
-   thickness,
-   curl,
-   length.

Established natural Valdari hair colors include:
- blonde,
- light brown,
- dark brown,
- black,
- moss green,
- silver.

The prevalence of these colors varies among regional Valdari populations and cultures rather than every color occurring with equal frequency everywhere.

For example, current development associates **blonde, light brown, and moss green** with forest Valdari populations, while plains Valdari populations may also include **dark brown and black** more commonly.

The exact regional distributions and frequencies remain **TBD** and should be developed alongside the individual Valdari cultures.

Hair color should not be treated as an absolute marker of cultural identity. Individual ancestry and population variation can produce Valdari whose coloration differs from what is typical within the region or culture where they live.

Braids are culturally common because they are practical and provide opportunities for decoration.

Flowers, herbs, beads, carved wooden pieces, ribbons, and other small objects may be woven into hairstyles.
Braided or curly hair is **not biologically required**.

Vaelyn's exceptionally long golden-blonde braid is an individual characteristic rather than the default appearance of Valdari women.

# 8. Skin

Valdari possess a naturally varied but recognizable range of human-like complexions. Their skin most commonly ranges from fair skin with warm peach undertones through warm beige, honeyed, and light-to-medium olive tones.

Freckles are common, particularly across the face, shoulders, and arms. Valdari who spend considerable time outdoors may develop noticeable tanning and sun-weathering.

Regional communities may differ in how frequently particular complexions appear, but no skin tone belongs exclusively to any Valdari culture or subculture. Individuals and families within the same community may possess different complexions, and cultural identity is primarily expressed through clothing, hairstyles, jewelry, customs, and other traditions rather than skin color.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Body Types & Individual Variation

There is no single "Valdari build."

The typical Valdari is relatively small-framed and naturally agile, but individuals vary according to genetics, occupation, lifestyle, age, and health.

Adult Valdari may be:

-   slender,
-   athletic,
-   soft-bodied,
-   curvaceous,
-   muscular,
-   broad for their species,
-   narrow-framed,
-   heavier or lighter.

Their small stature should never eliminate ordinary biological diversity.

### Typical Physical Character

As a population, Valdari tend toward:

-   good balance,
-   dexterity,
-   endurance,
-   agility,
-   precise movement,
-   relatively light frames.

They generally rely more upon coordination and endurance than brute strength.

A Valdari blacksmith, farmer, warrior, scholar, and venerable herbalist should not possess identical physiques.

# 10. Sexual Dimorphism

Male and female Valdari share the same basic racial proportions and youthful facial qualities.

Adult males are typically somewhat taller than females. 

Typical adult females stand approximately 3’0” (91 cm), while typical adult males stand approximately 3’2” (97 cm). Individual male and female heights overlap considerably within the absolute adult height range.

### Typical Adult Female

An adult Valdari woman possesses fully mature feminine anatomy appropriate to her individual body type.

She may have:

-   developed breasts,
-   a defined or subtle waist,
-   rounded or narrow hips,
-   varying amounts of muscle and body fat.

### Typical Adult Male

An adult Valdari man possesses fully mature masculine anatomy.

Males may commonly show:

-   somewhat broader shoulders,
-   somewhat narrower hips,
-   greater average muscle mass,
-   slightly greater average height.

These differences should remain natural rather than exaggerated.

A typical Valdari male should **not** resemble a miniature human bodybuilder.

# 12. Hands & Feet

Valdari hands are relatively small but highly dexterous.

Quick and precise hand movements are characteristic of a people whose
traditional work frequently involves:

-   herb preparation,
-   writing,
-   alchemy,
-   woodworking,
-   gardening,
-   weaving,
-   instruments,
-   delicate tools,
-   spell components.

Their feet remain proportionate to their bodies.

They **do not possess oversized or hairy Hobbit-like feet**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Movement

Valdari movement tends to be quiet, balanced, and deliberate.

Their bodies are particularly suited to navigating:

-   uneven woodland terrain,
-   roots,
-   rocks,
-   narrow paths,
-   streams,
-   gardens,
-   dense vegetation.

Grace should not be confused with fragility.

Valdari are resilient people accustomed to physical activity.

Their strength tends to manifest through endurance, balance, dexterity, and efficient movement rather than raw power.

# 14. Childhood & Development

Valdari develop through distinct stages of infancy, childhood, adolescence, and adulthood.

Their development is **not** simply human development slowed in direct proportion to their exceptionally long lifespan.

The distinction between children, adolescents, and adults is particularly important because adult Valdari retain youthful facial characteristics throughout life.

## Infancy & Early Childhood

Valdari young are born dependent upon their caregivers and are nursed by their mothers.

Other foods are introduced progressively during early development rather than nursing remaining the child's exclusive source of nutrition throughout the entire nursing period.

Young Valdari are typically fully weaned at approximately **3--3½ years of age --- Working Canon**.

## Childhood

Valdari remain biologically children until approximately **20 years of age --- Working Canon**.

During childhood they possess genuinely immature body proportions and remain reproductively immature.

Prepubescent Valdari children should have:

-   undeveloped secondary sex characteristics,
-   minimal muscular definition,
-   straighter torsos,
-   proportionally larger heads,
-   shorter limbs,
-   softer body contours,
-   clearly immature skeletal proportions.

Childhood does not imply helplessness or lack of learning. Children progressively gain physical coordination, practical skills, knowledge, responsibilities, and independence as they grow.

For detailed family upbringing and childhood education, see [[Valdari Culture & Daily Life Bible]].

## Adolescence / Pre-Adulthood

At approximately **20 years of age**, a Valdari begins the prolonged transition from childhood toward adulthood.

This adolescent or pre-adult period lasts approximately three decades.

During this period:

-   childhood proportions progressively give way to adult proportions,
-   skeletal and muscular development continues,
-   secondary sex characteristics develop,
-   physical capability increases,
-   the individual may undertake serious work,
-   professional or magical training may become increasingly advanced,
-   and the individual may carry meaningful responsibilities within the community.

A Valdari in their thirties or forties should therefore **not** be understood as a child simply because they have not yet reached full adulthood.

Late-adolescent Valdari may be physically capable, highly trained, professionally competent, and entrusted with serious work while their biological maturation is still approaching completion.

Sexual development and attraction may emerge progressively during adolescence. Reproductive maturity is typically reached later in adolescence, at approximately 35–40 years of age.

## Adulthood

Valdari reach full biological adulthood at approximately **45-50 years of age --- Working Canon**.

At adulthood they possess:

-   mature skeletal proportions,
-   mature secondary sex characteristics,
-   adult muscle and fat distribution,
-   adult posture and movement,
-   fully developed bodies.

Valdari culture recognizes this transition into adulthood. Some form of coming-of-age or adulthood-recognition ceremony is established in principle, while its exact customs remain to be developed in [[Valdari Culture & Daily Life Bible]].

Reaching adulthood does **not** necessarily coincide with completing professional training, beginning adult-level work, or leaving the family household.

Those are cultural and occupational matters rather than biological requirements for adulthood.

The **face remains characteristically youthful**.

The **body is fully mature**.

# 15. Aging & Lifespan
The Valdari are exceptionally long-lived but **mortal**.

Their current working natural lifespan is approximately:

## **1,000 years --- Working Canon**

This supersedes the earlier working lifespan of approximately **350--450 years**.

Valdari mortality is genuine. A Valdari who avoids disease, injury, violence, and other premature causes of death will nevertheless eventually reach the natural end of their biological lifespan.

## Persistent Youthful Appearance

A defining biological characteristic of the Valdari is that they retain their extraordinarily youthful appearance throughout adulthood, including at venerable ages and near the natural end of life.

A Valdari of 900 years may therefore still possess:

-   smooth, youthful facial contours,
-   the characteristic youthful Valdari facial structure,
-   healthy-looking skin,
-   and an outward appearance that does not resemble conventional human old age.

Valdari do **not** normally develop a visibly elderly appearance comparable to aging humans.

Youthful appearance does not mean that biological aging is nonexistent.

## Venerable Age

Extremely old Valdari may experience subtle physical decline despite continuing to look youthful.

Possible manifestations include:

-   somewhat slower movement,
-   reduced stamina,
-   reduced physical resilience,
-   slower recovery from exertion,
-   and other understated effects of extreme biological age.

A venerable Valdari should not automatically be depicted as frail, stooped, deeply wrinkled, or otherwise conventionally elderly.

Even a Valdari approaching natural death retains the characteristic youthful appearance of their people.

Exactly how Valdari recognize extreme age in one another remains **TBD**.

The precise natural lifespan range surrounding the approximately 1,000-year norm also remains **TBD**.

# 16. Reproduction

> [!warning\] Working Biology
> Valdari reproductive biology is still under development. The following
> represents the current working model rather than immutable canon.

Valdari reproduction is fundamentally mammalian and broadly comparable to human reproduction.

Conception involves sexual reproduction between biologically compatible adults, followed by implantation and development within the uterus.

## Reproductive Maturity

Valdari do **not** become reproductively mature during childhood or pre-adolescence.

Sexual development and attraction may emerge progressively during adolescence.

Valdari attain reproductive maturity during adolescence at  approximately **35-40 years of age --- Working Canon**.

Sexual behavior and reproductive capability are therefore biologically distinct.

Cultural expectations surrounding adolescent relationships and sexual behavior remain **TBD** and belong primarily in [[Valdari Culture & Daily Life Bible]].

The exact reproductive lifespan and fertility rate remain **TBD**.

Because of the Valdari's extraordinary longevity, previous references to **high fertility** should not currently be considered canon.

Their long lifespan, relatively late reproductive maturity, approximately 18-month gestation, and extended period of infant nursing suggest a high-investment reproductive pattern, but exact conception frequency, child spacing, and population growth remain to be developed deliberately.

# 17. Pregnancy & Gestation

Current working canon places Valdari gestation at approximately:

## **18 months**

This distinguishes Valdari reproductive biology from humans without extending pregnancy to an impractical portion of adult life.

Development is not simply human gestation occurring at half speed.

Instead, early Valdari development appears comparatively slow and deliberate.

The first portion of pregnancy emphasizes:

-   cellular development,
-   organ formation,
-   neurological development,
-   sensory development,
-   maturation.

Physical growth becomes more pronounced during later stages.

This allows Valdari infants to remain appropriately small at birth despite their longer gestation.

Pregnancy generally progresses gradually, becoming increasingly apparent during the second half.

Multiple births are currently considered **rare --- Working Canon**.

# 18. Birth & Infancy

Exact newborn measurements remain **Working Canon**.

Current concept work suggests Valdari newborns are exceptionally small relative to human infants.

The extended gestation may result in infants that are neurologically and sensorily somewhat more developed at birth despite their small physical size.

Possible characteristics include:

-   strong sensory awareness,
-   rapid recognition of caregivers,
-   strong grasp reflex,
-   relatively alert behavior.

These traits require further biological development before becoming final canon.

## Nursing & Weaning

Valdari mothers nurse their young.

Other appropriate foods are introduced progressively as the child develops, with nursing gradually becoming supplementary rather than remaining the child's sole source of nutrition.

Complete weaning normally occurs at approximately **3--3½ years of age --- Working Canon**.

The relationship between nursing duration, maternal fertility, conception frequency, and typical spacing between children remains **TBD** and should be developed as part of Valdari reproductive biology.

# 19. Family & Children

Children are deeply valued within Valdari society.

Their long lives make childhood only one portion of a much larger lifespan, but this does not make childhood insignificant.

Children are raised within a strongly communal environment involving:

-   parents,
-   siblings,
-   extended family,
-   teachers or mentors where appropriate,
-   craftspeople,
-   elders,
-   neighbors.

Learning begins through observation, imitation, play, storytelling, and participation in ordinary community life.

Detailed childhood education, household structure, apprenticeship, and coming-of-age customs belong primarily in [[Valdari Culture & Daily Life Bible]].

# 20. Clothing

Valdari clothing is practical, durable, finely crafted, and designed for freedom of movement.

Common materials include:

-   linen,
-   wool,
-   leather,
-   woven plant fibers,
-   other locally available textiles.

Typical colors draw heavily from natural surroundings:

-   forest green,
-   moss,
-   brown,
-   cream,
-   bark,
-   muted gold,
-   subdued floral colors.

Decoration may include:

-   leaves,
-   flowers,
-   branches,
-   subtle vines,
-   geometric patterns,
-   magical knotwork,
-   guild or family motifs.

# 21. Footwear

Valdari wear footwear.

This is a firm visual rule.

Typical footwear consists of:

-   soft leather shoes,
-   ankle boots,
-   soft-soled woodland boots,
-   occupation-specific protective footwear.

Their footwear emphasizes quiet movement and flexibility.

### Never depict standard Valdari as:

-   barefoot by default,
-   possessing hairy feet,
-   wearing oversized Hobbit-style shoes.

Bare feet may occur contextually---bathing, sleeping, medical examination, etc.---but are not the cultural norm.

# 23. Culture

Valdari culture emphasizes:

-   patience,
-   learning,
-   memory,
-   craftsmanship,
-   observation,
-   stewardship,
-   community,
-   tradition,
-   curiosity.

They are not culturally stagnant.

Tradition preserves accumulated knowledge, but curiosity encourages experimentation and refinement.

The ideal Valdari is not someone who blindly repeats what ancestors did.

It is someone who understands **why** it was done before deciding whether it should continue.

Valdari culture tends to encourage curiosity, thoughtfulness, attentiveness, and careful observation. Noticing subtle details and recognizing patterns are widely respected abilities, though individual Valdari vary considerably in temperament and personality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Lifelong Learning

Education does not truly end among the Valdari.

A person may study several disciplines over centuries.

Foundational childhood learning occurs primarily through the home, family, and community before young Valdari gradually move toward apprenticeships, guild instruction, mentorship, or other forms of specialized study.

Complex professions can require decades of training.

Earlier development suggested approximate training periods such as:

-   martial disciplines: roughly a decade,
-   advanced magical disciplines: potentially 20--30 years,
-   skilled crafts: variable apprenticeship.

These are development examples rather than fixed universal requirements; exact systems remain under development.

Professional competence and biological adulthood are not identical milestones. A pre-adult Valdari may become capable of serious professional work before reaching adulthood at approximately 45-50, while some demanding fields of study may continue beyond adulthood.

Mastery carries considerable cultural weight because Valdari understand just how long true expertise can take.

For detailed education and apprenticeship structures, see [[Valdari Culture & Daily Life Bible]].

# 27. Alchemy & Herbcraft


Valdari alchemy is strongly connected to the natural world.

Alchemy may involve:
-   tinctures,
-   oils,
-   powders,
-   potions,
-   preserved essences,
-   fungi,
-   minerals,
-   plants,
-   magical reagents.

Herbcraft encompasses cultivation, identification, harvesting, preparation, and medicinal or magical use.

Neither discipline is inherently magical in every application.

A Valdari herbalist may simply possess generations of accumulated botanical knowledge.

# 28. Relationship With Nature

The Valdari do not view themselves as separate from nature.

Nor do they necessarily worship nature itself.

A forest is simultaneously:

-   home,
-   ecosystem,
-   history,
-   livelihood,
-   responsibility.

Taking from nature is not inherently wrong.

Taking thoughtlessly is.

Valdari commonly replant, cultivate, maintain, restore, and carefully manage the environments upon which they depend.

This philosophy extends beyond forests. Valdari settlements can exist along rivers, roads, agricultural regions, forest edges, and other environments.

They are **not restricted to deep woodland settlements**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 29. Settlements

Valdari communities vary considerably in size.

Small settlements may contain only a few families.

Larger towns may contain hundreds or thousands.

The largest known Valdari settlement is currently envisioned at approximately **3,000--5,000 inhabitants**.

Such a settlement might qualify as a city by population while looking nothing like a densely built human city.

Homes, workshops, gardens, shops, public spaces, schools or learning spaces where established, and natural areas intermingle.

Specialized areas may develop around military, scholarly, civic, or other functions, but ordinary Valdari settlements should not be divided into rigid fantasy "districts" where every profession occupies its own isolated quarter.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 30. The Elder Trees

Some important Valdari settlements are associated with enormous ancient trees.

One settlement concept includes a single almost mythically large **Elder Tree**, surrounded by several other gigantic ancient trees.

These trees predate the settlement.

The Valdari did not grow them specifically to become buildings.

Communities may develop around such trees because of their historical, cultural, spiritual, or magical significance.

### Important

Valdari do **not** normally build their houses inside trees.

Valdari settlements are **not treehouse cities**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 31. Architecture

> [!important] Superseded Material
> Earlier concepts described Valdari settlements as being *grown rather than built*, with living trees shaped into houses and workshops.
>
> **This is no longer current canon.**

Current Valdari architecture is deliberately constructed.

Major characteristics include:

-   stone foundations,
-   timber construction,
-   sophisticated wooden joinery,
-   approximately 1--1½-story residential scale,
-   restrained natural decoration,
-   practical construction adapted to climate and terrain.

Valdari woodworking may employ techniques conceptually reminiscent of sophisticated Japanese timber joinery: carefully fitted structural connections that rely upon exceptional craftsmanship rather than crude construction.

Buildings should look as though skilled Valdari carpenters actually constructed them.

They are **not**:

-   Hobbit holes,
-   generic woodland cottages,
-   mushroom houses,
-   fairy houses,
-   houses grown from living trees,
-   miniature human houses with no cultural identity.

For detailed construction standards, see [[Valdari Architectural Bible]].

# 32. Community Layout

Valdari communities tend to grow organically around:

-   roads,
-   rivers,
-   streams,
-   gardens,
-   ancient trees,
-   farmland,
-   workshops,
-   public gathering spaces.

Homes and businesses commonly intermingle.

A blacksmith may live near a gardener.

A scholar may live beside a baker.

A family home may stand across from a merchant.

This produces settlements that feel **lived in rather than artificially zoned**.

# 33. Government

Valdari communities are traditionally guided by respected Elders or councils.

Current concepts favor **council-based governance** rather than hereditary kingship.

Discussion and consensus carry significant cultural importance.

For a people accustomed to thinking in decades and centuries, decision-making may appear frustratingly slow to shorter-lived peoples.

The precise:

-   number of Elders,
-   method of selection,
-   terms of service,
-   authority,
-   relationship between settlements

remain **TBD**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 34. Groves

The term **Grove** has appeared historically in Valdari development.

Its exact canonical meaning should remain open until settlement terminology is finalized.

Earlier material treated a Grove simultaneously as village, extended
family, school, government, and spiritual center.

That concept may still inform Valdari community organization, but **Grove should not currently be treated as the mandatory name for every Valdari settlement**.

# 35. Relations With Other Peoples

## Humans

Relations are generally capable of being friendly, although human impatience can clash with Valdari deliberation.

A traditional humorous observation might be summarized as:

Humans believe Valdari spend too much time thinking.

Valdari believe humans begin acting before they have finished thinking.

## Dwarves

There is potential for strong mutual respect based upon craftsmanship.

Dwarven mastery of stone and metal contrasts naturally with Valdari traditions of wood, plants, and alchemy.

## Elves

The Valdari have been envisioned as distant **cousins to the elves**, though the exact biological, mythological, or historical relationship remains **TBD**.

The Valdari themselves are mortal. The exact nature and longevity of elves should not be inferred from Valdari biology and remains governed by its own lore.

## Murnari

The Murnari maintain an important relationship with Valdari communities through their nomadic caravans.

They may visit particular Valdari settlements once or twice per year depending upon road conditions, trade routes, weather, and circumstance.

The exact cultural relationship between the peoples remains under development.

# 36. Visual Canon

The following rules should govern future Valdari concept art unless this Race Bible is deliberately revised.

## ALWAYS

- Typical adult Valdari females stand approximately **3'0”.**
- Typical adult Valdari males stand approximately **3'2”.**
- These are typical population ranges; absolute adult Valdari height range is approximately **2’10”–3’4” (86–102 cm).**
- Vaelyn stands approximately **3'2” (97 cm),** she is taller than the average Valdari woman but not unusually large.
- Valdari eyes may naturally occur in shades of **teal, amber, blue, and green** and possess a clear, luminous, unusually vivid visual quality without literally glowing.
- Natural Valdari hair colors include **blonde, light brown, dark brown, black, moss green, and silver**, with regional frequencies varying among populations.
- Valdari skin pigmentation ranges broadly from **pale through varied earthen hues to very deep or ebony complexions**, with regional distributions still under development.
- Adults have **fully mature bodies**.
- Adults retain unusually **youthful faces throughout life**.
- Venerable Valdari do not automatically develop conventional human elderly appearance.
- Children have genuinely immature anatomy and proportions.
- Adolescents progressively develop adult proportions between childhood and full adulthood.
- Bodies use believable humanoid proportions.
- Ears are human-like with a **gentle backward-swept upper tip**.
- Hands and feet remain proportionate.
- Valdari normally wear soft shoes or boots.
- Adult physiques show natural variation.
- Clothing is practical and culturally crafted.
- Movement should suggest balance, dexterity, and quiet confidence.
- Adult characters should communicate adulthood through bearing and context even when their faces appear youthful.

# 37. Avoid in Future Artwork

Unless specifically requested for an unusual individual or circumstance, do **not** depict Valdari as:

-   generic halflings,
-   Hobbits,
-   gnomes,
-   miniature elves,
-   fairies,
-   human children,
-   chibi characters,
-   anime characters,
-   disproportionately large-headed,
-   stocky by default,
-   barefoot by default,
-   hairy-footed,
-   possessing long pointed elven ears,
-   living inside Hobbit holes,
-   living inside trees,
-   living primarily in treehouses,
-   wearing excessive vines simply because they are nature-oriented,
-   universally dressed as druids,
-   universally blonde,
-   universally green-eyed,
-   universally built like Vaelyn,
-   conventionally elderly in appearance solely because they are
    venerable in age.

# 38. Adult vs. Child --- Critical Visual Rule

This distinction must remain consistent in all future artwork.

### Valdari Child

**Youthful face + immature body**

### Valdari Adolescent

**Youthful face + progressively maturing body**

### Valdari Adult

**Youthful face + mature adult body**

### Venerable Valdari Adult

**Youthful face + mature adult body + potentially subtle age-related
reduction in physical vigor**

That is the fundamental visual distinction.

Small stature does not determine age.

Facial youth does not determine age.

The physical maturity of the body, posture, expression, clothing,
behavior, and social context must all contribute to communicating age.

# 41. Superseded Concepts

The following ideas appeared during earlier development but should **not** currently be treated as canon:

-   Valdari settlements are grown rather than constructed.
-   Houses are shaped from living trees.
-   Valdari primarily live within hollow trees or roots.
-   All settlements are hidden deep within forests.
-   Valdari ears are simply generic pointed fantasy ears.
-   All Valdari have the same slender body type.
-   Valdari are barefoot woodland folk.
-   Vaelyn's individual physical characteristics represent all Valdari females.
-   High fertility is an established racial characteristic.
-   Valdari have an average lifespan of approximately 350--450 years.
-   Valdari eventually develop a conventional visibly elderly appearance comparable to humans.

These concepts can be revisited intentionally, but should not accidentally re-enter the setting through older notes or concept-art prompts.

# 42. Design Philosophy

When creating a new Valdari character, settlement, occupation, or piece of artwork, the guiding question should not be:

> **"How would a small fantasy woodland person do this?"**

Instead ask:

> **"How would the Valdari do this?"**

Their identity comes from the combination of:

**small stature without childishness;\
youth without immaturity;\
longevity without immortality;\
nature without primitivism;\
magic without spectacle;\
tradition without ignorance;\
craftsmanship without industrialization;\
and community without uniformity.**

That combination is what makes someone recognizably **Valdari**.
