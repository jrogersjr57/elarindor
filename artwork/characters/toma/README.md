Store retained Toma artwork directly in this directory with descriptive, stable filenames. Record each image's status here rather than moving it among status subdirectories.

## Retained Artwork

- `toma-caravan-guard-loadout-exploration.png` — **Exploration** — First substantial visual study of Toma as a mobile Murnari caravan guard. Establishes the useful overall direction of practical integrated light protection, saber, throwing knives, and a mobile protector silhouette. Later discussion refined the equipment to a seven-knife bandolier plus 2–3 belt/boot backups, primarily left-handed saber use with right-handed throwing, and armor integrated directly into her travel clothing. Do not treat the image’s exact weapon placement, handedness, knife count, armor construction, or decorative details as authoritative.
